## 1. [P2] Person rename rollback reinstalls a stale shadow name after background reconciliation

Location: `frontend/src/mockups/prototype/store.tsx:2167` through the two state updates at lines 2170 and 2171; background source subscription at line 612.

The source subscription applies the authoritative journal name to `people` and `extraPeople` but leaves `personNameEditsRef` holding its previous successful name. That ref is pruned by `reconcilePersonName` during People fetches, but background reconciliation can update the rendered name before another full People fetch occurs. A subsequent failed rename walks the stale shadow chain and writes its old name directly into both state collections. `personById` reads these collections without applying the journal overlay, so the stale name becomes visible even though the journal correctly rolled back to the newer authoritative name.

Reproduced through StoreProvider, using the actual `faceService.labelPerson` implementation and actual background reconciliation service with controlled HTTP responses:

2. Successfully rename to First, creating the successful shadow entry and central saved receipt.
3. Another client has renamed the person to Remote. Background source reconciliation adopts Remote and updates the visible People list through the subscription. Do not perform another People fetch before the next edit.
4. Rename to Attempt and reject its save.
5. The journal correctly projects **Remote**, but `store.personById('p1').name` and `store.people[0].name` revert to **First**.

This is a remaining defect in the uncommitted person shadow-chain integration, not a regression caused by the latest payload-version comparison. The analogous album rollback now routes restored values through `applyPendingEdits`; person rollback still bypasses it. Resolve both person collections through the current journal after failure, or synchronize/prune the person shadow entry when authoritative source reconciliation changes the name.
